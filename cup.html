Need to know

League Cup questions.

Everything you need to know about qualification, scheduling, advancement, and prizes.

How do players qualify for the League Cup?＋

Qualification is determined by verified Elo ratings on the global rankings leaderboard at the qualification cutoff. The top 32 players earn direct seeding into the championship bracket.

How are bracket advancements recorded?＋

Match results are submitted by players through the Match Centre with scoreline screenshot verification. League administrators review and confirm the outcome, instantly updating the official knockout tree.

What platforms are supported?＋

The League Cup supports cross-platform competition across Mobile, Console (PlayStation/Xbox), and PC. Match lobbies utilize the in-game friend match feature with standard competitive settings.

What rewards does the League Cup Champion receive?＋

The champion receives the official Season 04 League Cup trophy badge on their verified profile, priority seeding for upcoming invitational majors, and peak Elo points towards season rankings.
