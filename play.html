New to eFootball?

Learn the rhythm of the game.

Get comfortable with the fundamentals before you compete. Button layouts change by platform and update, so use the in-game command list for the exact controls on your device.

⌖

01 / BUILD UP

Keep the ball moving

Use short passes to keep possession, look for space before sending a through pass, and avoid forcing a risky pass through a crowded area.

➤

02 / ATTACK

Create a clear chance

Move into space, choose the right moment to shoot, and use sprint or skill actions selectively. Rushing every attack can leave you exposed.

⛨

03 / DEFEND

Stay goal-side

Switch to a defender, track the runner, and close space before attempting a tackle. Charging out too early can open a lane to goal.

▤

04 / MANAGE

Think beyond the ball

Choose a formation that suits you, watch player stamina, and make substitutions or tactical changes when the game situation calls for them.

✦Best way to practise: Try the game’s training or practice mode first. Learn one new command at a time, then use it in a friendly before a ranked fixture.

From lobby to final whistle

How a league match flows.

Keep communication simple and make sure both players are on the same page at every stage.

01

Connect

Join using the platform and room instructions supplied by your organiser. Double-check your opponent before you begin.

02

Confirm

Before kick-off, confirm the competition, match length, settings, and any event-specific requirements together.

03

Compete

Play the full agreed fixture. If there is a technical problem, pause and contact your opponent or organiser rather than guessing what to do.

04

Close it out

Agree the final score, keep any evidence your event requires, then submit the result using the organiser’s stated process.

The league standard

Compete hard.
Play fair.

Great competition is built on clear agreements and respect. These are the basics for every player.

01

Respect the agreement

Follow the confirmed time, format, and event rules. If you need to change plans, tell your opponent as early as possible.

02

Keep it sportsmanlike

Be respectful in messages and in-game. No harassment, threats, cheating, exploiting, or deliberately disrupting play.

03

Be honest with results

Report only matches that were played. Never alter, invent, or pressure someone to accept a score they do not agree with.

04

Keep your details safe

Keep your player and contact details private, and contact the organiser if you think someone else has accessed them.

    Common match-day questions.

    If an issue could affect a result, pause and contact your event organiser before taking action.

    What if my opponent is late or does not respond?＋

    Send a polite message using the agreed channel and keep a record of your attempts. Follow your event’s deadline and contact its organiser. Do not claim a win or report a result unless the event rules explicitly tell you what to do.

    What should I do if the game disconnects?＋

    Message your opponent and organiser as soon as possible. Share what happened and any requested evidence, then wait for instructions. Whether to replay, resume, or record a result depends on the event rules.

    We disagree about the score. What now?＋

    Do not submit a result you believe is incorrect. Keep the conversation respectful, save relevant evidence if allowed, and ask the organiser to review the disagreement.

    Which teams and match settings should we use?＋

    Use the settings specified by your competition. If the rules do not say, ask the organiser and agree with your opponent before starting. Do not assume settings used in another event apply here.

    Do I need special controls or a particular console?＋

    Use a supported device and control setup for your version of eFootball. Controls and available features differ across platforms and updates, so check the game’s own settings and the event’s eligibility rules.

    How do I join a tournament or see my ranking?＋

    Browse published tournaments for event information and use the rankings page to see current standings based on verified player profiles and approved ranked results.
