Community standards

Terms of use

Last updated: November 2026

Using the league

Use an account that belongs to you, keep your sign-in details private, and provide accurate player information. You must be able to receive email at the address on your account to verify it and receive match invitations.

Matches and results

Only report scores that were played. Follow the match rules, provide genuine screenshot evidence when requested, and respond honestly to disputes or walkover claims. Approved ranked results may update player ratings and records.

Respectful play

Do not harass other players, manipulate results, impersonate another player, or misuse reporting tools. Administrators may review reports and restrict accounts that violate these standards.

Account actions

You may ask an administrator to correct profile information or delete your account. League records may be retained where needed to preserve the integrity of completed competitions and resolve disputes.

Service changes

Features and competition schedules may change. Published event details and match-specific rules apply to the relevant competition. Contact the league administrator through the website for help.
