Privacy policy

Last updated: November 2026

Information we store

Your account includes your name, email address, unique gamer tag, platform, country, time zone, verification and security data, and any club assignment. Match invitations, private match-chat messages, submitted scores, screenshots, disputes, and approved results are stored to operate competitions.

What is public

Verified player profiles may display gamer tag, rating, approved record, platform, country, and club membership. If a player adds a stream link, that HTTPS link and the match participants’ gamer tags and scheduled time are shown publicly after the opponent accepts. Email addresses, passwords, private match links, private chat, and submitted evidence are not shown on public pages.

How information is used

We use account and match information to verify players, send invitations and account emails, calculate rankings, administer events, review disputes, and respond to abuse reports. Private match chat is available only to the two participants and is not included in public match listings. Match screenshots are restricted to authorized reviewers.

Administrator access and retention

Authorized administrators can edit player records and published league content. Account and competition records are retained while needed to operate the league, maintain rankings, and resolve disputes. Contact the league administrator to request access, correction, or account deletion.

Security

Passwords are stored as salted hashes, and private routes require authentication or a scoped match link. No online service can guarantee absolute security; do not share your password or private match links.
