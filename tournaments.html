League Operations

Tournament Rules & FAQ

How are tournament seeds determined?＋

Tournament seeding directly reflects your verified Elo rating on the leaderboard at the qualification cutoff date. Top seeds receive favorable bracket placements.

What happens if my opponent disconnects or fails to show?＋

Matches carry a 15-minute forfeit grace period. Take a screenshot showing your active lobby and message support through the Match Centre to request a walkover decision.

Are tournament fixtures ranked?＋

Yes. All official tournament bracket fixtures utilize our verified K=32 Elo calculation engine, directly impacting your global player ranking.

Can I participate in multiple competitions concurrently?＋

Yes, provided scheduled kickoff times do not conflict. You can track all your active commitments under your profile dashboard.
