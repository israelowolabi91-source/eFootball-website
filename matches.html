Match-day support

A few things worth knowing.

These pages are a preview centre, not a live fixture or score-reporting service.

Are these fixtures and results real?＋

No. Match names, dates, pairings, times, and scores here are illustrative preview content. They are not connected to live league data and should not be used to plan or report an actual match.

How do I find my official fixture?＋

Check the information supplied by your tournament or league organiser and confirm the time and details directly through the official channel they provide. The fixture list on this page is not an official schedule.

Does “Save” send me a reminder?＋

No. Save keeps an example fixture in this browser on this device only. Real accepted challenges in your player profile can send a reminder email to both players before kick-off.

How are challenge scores and rankings handled?＋

Both players submit the score and a screenshot. The owner reviews both reports and evidence before approving the result. Only approved ranked matches update the Elo leaderboard; friendly results do not change ratings.

What if my opponent does not show up or the game disconnects?＋

Keep communication respectful, note what happened, and contact the organiser promptly. Do not award yourself a result or replay a fixture unless the relevant competition rules tell you what to do.
