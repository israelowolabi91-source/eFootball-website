Match-day support

A few things worth knowing.

Match Centre actions require a verified account. Approved results are displayed from league records.

When does a result appear here?＋

Results are listed after they are approved and saved against the participating player records. Pending and disputed reports are not shown publicly.

How do I find my fixture?＋

Sign in and open your invitations and scheduled matches in the Match Centre. Private fixture details are available only to the participating players.

How do scores and rankings work?＋

Players submit the result and screenshot evidence. Only an approved ranked match changes Elo; friendly matches do not affect ratings.

What if my opponent does not show up or the game disconnects?＋

Keep communication respectful, record what happened, and report it through the available match controls. Do not submit a score that was not played.
